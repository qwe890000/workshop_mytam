## Detailed Content

### 1. The AI Paradox and the Explosion of Software Demand

Speaker brought an insightful perspective on the paradox of technology becoming cheaper: when a technology becomes more affordable, its demand skyrockets.

**Real-world example from LED light bulbs:**
- LED bulbs save 90% electricity compared to incandescent bulbs
- However, total electricity consumption for lighting did not decrease - it actually increased dramatically
- Why? Because humans expanded lighting to everywhere possible

**Applying this logic to AI and software development:**
- When AI makes software development faster, more efficient, and cheaper
- The number of software products created in the future will explode
- Evidence from Hackathon competitions (like Anthropic Hackathon):
  - Top 3 winners are not only IT professionals but also doctors, lawyers (non-IT people)
  - This proves that AI has democratized software creation

### 2. Job Market and Recruitment Trends

**Short-term transition period:**
- Current market appears challenging due to macroeconomic influences
- Large tech companies are racing to invest heavily in AI hardware infrastructure
- Companies are looking for ways to optimize costs

**Long-term trends:**
- Software engineering-related jobs will have long-term growth
- **New emerging positions include:**
  - **Software Engineering**: Specializing in refining, debugging source code, and completing MVP products
  - **Platform Engineering**: Operating and maintaining complex systems

**Real perspective from large enterprises (Banking industry):**
- AI competency (AI competency) is a huge plus (must-have)
- However, when selecting candidates, employers fundamentally return to evaluating:
  - Solid software engineering fundamentals
  - Backend skills (such as secure password encoding/decoding in databases)

### 3. The Importance of Infrastructure, Tools, and Context

**Terraform (Infrastructure as Code - IaC):**
- Instead of manual operations on interfaces (click engineer) - which makes it difficult to track who changed what resources
- Modern enterprises use Terraform to write infrastructure deployment code
- Benefits:
  - Track change history through versions on repository
  - Easily control differences between versions
  - Quickly and safely duplicate (duplicate) infrastructure to different regions

**Context factors when applying AI:**
- Using general AI models alone is not enough
- Why? Because AI cannot inherently understand:
  - Company-specific rules and compliance requirements
  - Proprietary architecture of each company or individual project
- Technical professionals must know how to provide the right context and actual organizational data

### 4. Core Message for Building Systems

summarized the most important philosophy when developing systems through three criteria:

| Criteria | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Secure** | Ensure security control standards, protect systems from threats |
| **Reliable** | Build stable systems, ensure audit trails and logging |
| **User-centric** | Protect end users to the maximum in the AI era |

> Software is not just created to run, but must meet standards of security control, audit logging, and maximum protection for end users.

## Lessons Learned

Through the event, I gained valuable insights:

- **Technology affordability drives adoption:** Understanding the paradox helps predict future software demand

- **Context is king:** AI is powerful, but providing the right context determines success
- **Security and reliability are non-negotiable:** In the AI era, systems must be secure, reliable, and user-focused
